Video

Choose a video to preview its frames on the 8 × 8 grid. Slow MIDI output drops stale frames.

Fit preserves the frame's aspect ratio; Fill crops; Stretch distorts. Preview plays locally. Play on Launchpad streams sampled frames when connected; slower MIDI output skips frames to avoid lag. Video files stay on your device.

Static image

Choose an image, or paste one from the clipboard, and pick a sizing mode for the square grid.

Fit preserves aspect ratio within the grid; Fill crops to cover; Stretch distorts to fill. Colors are sampled to an 8 × 8 grid and sent to the Launchpad when connected. Files are read locally and are not uploaded.

Pad colors

Enter a hex value, three RGB numbers, or a named HTML color for each pad.

Examples: #00ff00, 00FF00, 0, 255, 0, darkred. RGB channels range from 0 to 255. Valid colors are sent as you edit.
